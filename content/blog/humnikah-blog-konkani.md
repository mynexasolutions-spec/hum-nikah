## Who are Konkani Muslims?

Konkani Muslims, also written Kokani or Kokni Muslims, are a community from the Konkan coast of Maharashtra. Families are found in Mumbai and Thane and in the districts of Palghar, Raigad, Ratnagiri and Sindhudurg, and many live in other cities and abroad. Many families speak a Konkani or Konkan Marathi dialect at home, along with Urdu, Marathi and English. The community has a long history of trade and seafaring along the Arabian Sea. Source: [Konkani Muslims on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konkani_Muslims).

Konkani Muslims are not one uniform group. Families differ in practice, in language at home and in expectations, so always ask each family about its own background rather than assuming.

## How families search for Konkani Muslim matrimony in Mumbai

Most families use more than one route:

- **Relatives and community networks.** Trusted and personal, but limited to the people your relatives know.
- **Local jamaats and mosques.** Useful for introductions and for checking a family's reputation.
- **Online matrimony sites.** Wide reach, but you search alone and profile quality varies.
- **A marriage bureau.** A matchmaker shortlists, introduces families and keeps the process private.

Many families keep their relatives' network open and add a bureau for privacy and screening. The bureau does not replace your own enquiries. It makes the search more organised and less tiring.

## Looking for a Konkani Muslim bride or groom: tips for both families

A Konkani Muslim shaadi begins with a good rishta, and a good rishta begins with clear, respectful communication.

### If you are the groom's family

- Be honest about what you are looking for and about your own family.
- Approach the bride's family through relatives or a bureau, not by messaging the girl directly.
- If you are looking for a Konkani Muslim bride, ask respectfully about education, work plans and religious practice, and listen to what the other side wants.
- Do not ask for or hint at dowry. Talk about mahr openly.

### If you are the bride's family

- Involve the Wali from the start.
- Ask about the groom's work, family, home and plans after marriage, and ask for references.
- Keep your daughter's photographs private until both families are serious.
- If you are looking for a Muslim Konkani girl's match, share your expectations clearly, so the shortlist is relevant.

## Are Konkani Muslims Sunni?

Most Konkani Muslims are Sunni, and many follow the Shafi'i school of thought (see [Konkani Muslims on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konkani_Muslims)). But families differ in practice and in which scholars they follow, so do not assume. If this matters to you, tell your matchmaker at the start, so the shortlist reflects it.

## Konkani Muslim wedding: what to agree on early

There is no single "Konkani Muslim wedding". Customs differ between villages, families and cities, so we do not describe one fixed set of rituals. What matters is that both families agree early on the main points:

- **Mahr:** the amount and form, which belongs to the bride
- **Events:** which events there will be, such as the engagement and the Nikah, and who hosts them
- **Guests and budget:** guest numbers and costs, so neither family is under pressure
- **Home:** where the couple will live after marriage
- **Gifts:** how each family wants to handle gifts, without any demand for dowry

Agreeing these points before the families meet formally saves misunderstandings later.
