## Busy schedules and how to plan the search

One of the greatest hurdles for tech professionals is managing time during the rishta process. Browsing matrimonial apps for hours every evening rarely leads to genuine results and often causes decision fatigue.

To streamline your search:
- **Delegate initial shortlisting:** Working with a trusted matchmaking bureau allows a coordinator to screen profiles against your specific education and career criteria, so you only evaluate serious, pre-screened proposals.
- **Set dedicated family time:** Reserve specific times during the week—such as Saturday mornings or Sunday afternoons—with your parents and elders to review proposals together.
- **Involve parents early:** Having parents or the bride's Wali initiate introductory calls with the other family saves young professionals from awkward cold messaging and ensures respect from the start.

## Be clear about location and work plans

In the IT sector, work arrangements can evolve rapidly. Clarity regarding your current setup and future mobility is essential:

- **Work modes:** Are you working fully from office, on a hybrid schedule, or permanently remote? If your company requires office presence in Bangalore, clarify whether you plan to remain in the city long-term.
- **Relocation possibilities:** Does your role involve opportunities to transfer to international tech hubs in the Gulf, Europe, or North America? Discuss whether you expect your future spouse to relocate with you.
- **Commute and housing:** Bangalore traffic across tech corridors (such as Outer Ring Road or Silk Board) can make living location a decisive factor. Discuss where in the city your household can be practically established.

## Expectations about careers after marriage

Modern tech couples value mutual support and personal development. Clear alignment on career goals prevents future friction:

- **Work-life balance:** How do you both intend to balance sprint deadlines, on-call shifts, or business travel with home life?
- **Spouse's career ambitions:** Discuss mutual expectations regarding the bride continuing her professional career or pursuing higher education, both before and after having children.
- **Support systems:** Consider how family support or household assistance will be organized to maintain harmony at home.

When both partners share mutual respect for each other's career goals, married life is built on strong partnership and peace.

## Keep your biodata short and private

A common mistake among tech professionals is creating an overly technical biodata that reads like an engineering resume. A marriage biodata should be concise, human, and focused on compatibility.

Best practices for professional biodatas:
- Summarize your qualifications clearly (e.g., "B.Tech in Computer Science, Software Engineer at a leading tech firm in Bangalore") without listing programming languages or niche project acronyms.
- State your personal lifestyle values, hobbies, and family background openly.
- Protect your corporate privacy: avoid publishing your exact corporate email address, employee ID, or internal team name on widely shared documents.
