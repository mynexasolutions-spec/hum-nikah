## Why fees differ between bureaus

The cost of matrimonial services in Bangalore depends largely on how much human time and manual effort the bureau invests in your file. 

Some operators function essentially as contact sellers. They collect biodatas into loose folders or unvetted spreadsheets and charge families simply to hand over a list of phone numbers. Because very little time is spent checking details, speaking with guardians, or filtering out outdated profiles, their initial registration fees may appear low, though the quality of introductions is often poor.

Other bureaus provide an end-to-end matchmaking service. In a full-service model, a dedicated matchmaker reads your family's preferences, verifies government identity and residential documents, speaks directly with parents and the bride's Wali, and coordinates respectful introductions. The time spent checking credentials, mediating discussions, and keeping profiles confidential accounts for the differences in cost between bureaus.

Location and overheads also play a role. An established bureau with a physical office—such as our location on Wheeler Road in Cox Town—offers families a safe, accountable place to meet in person, unlike online-only listings that may disappear without notice.

## What a fee should and should not cover

Before handing over any payment, ask the bureau for an itemized explanation of what their fee covers. 

A fair and professional matrimonial fee should cover:
- Manual screening of biodatas, including checking government identification and residence proofs.
- Time spent understanding your family's expectations regarding education, profession, language, and community background.
- Personalized shortlisting, so that you only receive profiles that match your stated criteria.
- Involving the bride's Wali and coordinating family introductions with privacy.
- Administrative communication and follow-ups with both sides to gather polite feedback.

A fee should never cover:
- False promises of guaranteed marriages or fixed wedding timelines.
- Access to private contact details or photographs without the explicit consent of the other family.
- Unregulated placement into public WhatsApp groups where phone numbers are exposed to strangers.
- Hidden administrative charges that were not disclosed in writing at the beginning.

## Questions to ask before paying

Before paying any registration or matchmaking fee in Bangalore, sit down with the coordinator and ask these four essential questions:

1. **Do you provide written terms and a proper receipt?** Any reputable marriage bureau will provide a clear written agreement detailing what services are included, along with a formal GST receipt or payment invoice. Never make cash payments without written acknowledgement.
2. **What are your refund and cancellation terms?** Ask what happens if you find a rishta through family channels or decide to pause your search. A transparent service clearly states whether fees are non-refundable, partially refundable, or transferable.
3. **What exactly is verified, and what is not?** Understand the limits of their checks. A reliable bureau checks government IDs and addresses, but will openly tell you that they do not run criminal background checks and that your family must do its own community enquiries. See our [checklist to verify a Muslim rishta](/blog/how-to-verify-a-muslim-rishta) for practical steps.
4. **Who is my contact person?** You should know the specific relationship manager handling your file, their phone number, and when they are available to discuss updates.

## Red flags to watch for

Be cautious and slow down if you encounter any of the following warning signs when speaking with a bureau in Bangalore:

- **High-pressure sales tactics:** If a broker demands immediate payment on the spot or claims a "perfect proposal" will be lost if you do not pay today, step away. Genuine matchmaking takes patience.
- **Guarantees of marriage:** No human can guarantee a match. Bureau operators who promise a guaranteed nikah within 30 or 60 days are making misleading claims.
- **Refusal to put terms in writing:** If terms, prices, or refund policies change verbally between conversations, do not proceed.
- **Vague screening explanations:** If a bureau claims their profiles are "100% verified" but cannot explain their document review process, their claims are unreliable.
- **Demands for sensitive financial details:** A legitimate bureau only requires identity, address, and profile details. They never ask for bank account access, ATM pins, or credit card passwords.
